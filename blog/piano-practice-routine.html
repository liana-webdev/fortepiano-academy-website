Building a Practice Routine That Actually Works

A good piano practice routine is specific. Students should know what to play, how to listen, and what improvement they are aiming for each day.

Short, focused practice is often better than a long session without clear goals. Beginners can start with small daily tasks, while intermediate students need more deliberate technical and musical work.

Separate the work

A useful routine includes technical exercises, new reading, focused work on difficult bars, polished repertoire, and quick review. Keeping those tasks separate makes progress easier to measure.

Use lesson notes

Written goals help families understand what should happen between lessons. Practice becomes less about repeating a piece and more about solving specific musical problems.

Need a clearer practice plan?

Structured weekly lesson notes can help students practise with purpose.
