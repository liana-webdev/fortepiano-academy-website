The Three Pianist Brothers A family journey through AMEB grades with focus and musical maturity.

Angus, the eldest, represents what steady dedication looks like over time.

His progress was shaped not only by repetition, but by understanding — learning to break down challenges, work through them patiently, and truly grasp what he was playing.

With a sharp ear and attention to detail, mistakes were never left to settle into habit. Instead, they were noticed, addressed, and refined immediately, allowing his playing to remain clean, controlled, and intentional. Over time, this developed into a deeper awareness — not just of notes, but of expression, emotion, and meaning behind the music.

His journey reflects something important:
true progress is not just in playing more difficult pieces, but in playing with clarity, control, and understanding.

Together, the three brothers form something more than individual stories.

Frequently Asked Questions

When can students start lessons?

Students can start at any time during the year. Fortepiano Academy operates on rolling enrolment rather than fixed school terms. Each student is placed into the appropriate level and program cycle based on their ability and goals.

Is the program tied to school terms?

No. Programs are grade-based rather than calendar-based. This allows students to begin when they are ready and progress without being restricted by school term dates.

How do AMEB exams fit into the program?

AMEB exam preparation is integrated into the curriculum for students following an examination pathway. Preparation begins well in advance, ensuring students are confident and ready regardless of the final exam date allocated by AMEB.

When are AMEB enrolments submitted?

Fortepiano Academy manages AMEB exam enrolments during official enrolment windows. Exam fees are covered for Development Program students; any other exam-related fees are explained during enrolment.

Does my child have to do an exam?

No. AMEB exams are optional. Students may follow an exam pathway or focus on musicianship, technique, repertoire, and performance without sitting formal examinations.

How is lesson length decided?

Lesson length is determined after an initial assessment, based on the student’s age, level, learning pace, and goals. As students progress, lesson length may increase to ensure sufficient time for technical work and repertoire.

What makes Fortepiano Academy different?

Fortepiano Academy offers structured programs with clear progression, disciplined technical training influenced by Russian pedagogy, regular progress tracking, and a supportive learning environment combined with the flexibility of the AMEB syllabus.

Can students join mid-year and still prepare for exams?

Yes. Depending on the student’s starting level, consistency, and commitment, it is often possible to prepare for an exam within a single program cycle. Most of our Development Program students complete their early grades within half a year with A marks.

How do I enrol myself or my child in piano lessons?

Enrolment begins with a short conversation via call or message to discuss the student’s age, experience, goals, and availability. An initial assessment lesson is then booked.

At what age can my child start piano lessons?

Children can begin lessons from around 4 years old, depending on their focus, coordination, and readiness. Piano lessons also support the development of concentration, listening skills, and fine motor control.

Do you prepare students for performances and exams?

Yes. Students are encouraged to participate in studio performances, recitals, and AMEB examinations.

What if my child has a busy schedule with other activities?

Flexible scheduling is available where possible. For busy students, more frequent lessons are often recommended, as they reduce reliance on long independent practice sessions and help maintain steady progress.

How often should lessons be scheduled, and how much practice is required?

Twice-weekly lessons are recommended for optimal progress. Practice expectations vary by level:

  • Beginners: approximately 10–20 minutes per day
  • Intermediate to advanced students: approximately 40–60 minutes per day

Consistent practice is essential for meaningful progress.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations are requested with at least 24 hours’ notice wherever possible. Each student has one charge-free cancellation per term, usable even with late notice. After this is used, cancellations with 24 hours’ notice are eligible for a make-up lesson or credit (subject to availability). Day-of cancellations and no-shows are forfeited. Make-ups and credits must be used within the same term and cannot be carried over.
