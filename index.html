How the Fortepiano System Works

Assessment → Term Planning → Progress Tracking → Exam/Recital

Every journey begins with an assessment lesson to evaluate skills and goals. The teacher then recommends the most suitable program pathway.

Download the guide and discover how Fortepiano Academy turns lessons into lasting success — built upon years of teaching and student achievement.

Programs

Two structured pathways designed to support different levels of commitment, pace, and responsibility.
All students begin with an assessment and are placed into the program best suited to their goals.

Foundation

Flexible, steady progression with shared responsibility.

FORMAT
1 lesson per week
BEST FOR
  • Casual learners
  • Families with tighter schedules
  • Students progressing at a relaxed pace
INCLUDES
  • Weekly practice diary
  • Term-based learning plan
  • End-of-term progress report
  • Participation in studio recitals
  • Limited support outside lesson hours
SUMMARY
A stable entry point into piano study, ideal for families seeking steadiness. Maximum flexibility, but much greater reliance on consistent home practice.
Book Assessment

Development

The core Fortepiano Academy program: reliable, structured, and results-focused.

FORMAT
2 lessons per week
BEST FOR
  • Exam-oriented students
  • Families seeking predictable, measurable progress
  • Students aiming to complete grades efficiently
INCLUDES

Everything in Foundation, plus:

  • Two weekly lessons (with frequency-based value)
  • Monthly progress reports
  • Structured AMEB exam preparation
  • Mock exams and readiness checks
  • Teacher-managed exam logistics
  • Outside-lesson support on demand (text/video support upon discussion and availability)
SUMMARY
The most comprehensive and recommended pathway for serious students and families who value structure, oversight, and consistency.
